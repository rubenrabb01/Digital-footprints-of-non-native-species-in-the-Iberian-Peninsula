# Licence options

The repository can be released under either a standard open licence or a more restrictive source-available notice, depending on the intended reuse conditions and institutional requirements.

## Open-reuse option

For maximum reuse and citation, a standard software licence such as MIT, GPL, or Apache 2.0 can be applied to code, together with a data licence such as CC BY 4.0 for reusable derived data and documentation.

## Restricted-reuse option

If the repository is intended primarily for manuscript reproducibility and inspection, but not for unrestricted reuse or derivative package development, a custom source-available or all-rights-reserved notice may be more appropriate. This option should be checked against journal policy and, where relevant, institutional or legal guidance.

## Zenodo note

The Zenodo licence should match the intended reuse terms of the repository. The default Zenodo licence should be reviewed before archiving the release.
