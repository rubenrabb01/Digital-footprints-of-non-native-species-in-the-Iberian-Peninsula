Choose the final license before public release.

If you want maximum reuse and citation: use a standard software license such as MIT/GPL and a data license such as CC-BY 4.0.
If you want reproducibility but do not want to grant reuse rights for future package development: consider a custom source-available / all-rights-reserved notice after checking journal policy and, if needed, institutional/legal advice.

Do not leave Zenodo's default license unchanged unless it matches your intended permissions.
